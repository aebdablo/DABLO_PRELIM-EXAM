### Presentation Design Principles

> *Sometimes, the best designs begin with something simple.*

This activity was my first step into the world of intentional visual communication. Using a free coffee advertisement as my creative concept, I explored how presentation and design principles can transform an ordinary message into something visually engaging and easy to understand.

The goal was not simply to make a coffee poster look attractive. I learned to think about how visual elements work together: where the viewer's attention should go first, how typography can establish hierarchy, how images can strengthen a message, and how spacing and composition can create a balanced visual experience.

My final output combines the coffee concept with a promotional visual designed to immediately communicate its message while maintaining a warm, inviting atmosphere.

### ✦ What I explored

* Visual hierarchy
* Alignment and composition
* Typography
* Balance and spacing
* Image placement
* Audience-focused communication

### ✦ My creative takeaway

This activity taught me that **good design is not decoration; it is communication with intention.** Every element needs a reason to be there, and every design choice contributes to how the audience receives the message.

This activity moved beyond designing a single visual and introduced me to something more intentional: **building an identity**.

# ⋆｡°✩ ACTIVITY 03 — TURNING INFORMATION INTO IMPACT ✩°｡⋆

### Urban Crisis: Transit Delays & Commuter Congestion

<div align="center">

<img width="100%" height="500" alt="GE 4120 PRELIMS_FINDING THE MESSAGE" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/608b1641-23d2-4fc9-9593-71b926725d55" />

</div>

> *When information becomes visual, complexity becomes easier to understand.*

For this activity, I shifted from purely aesthetic design toward **information-driven visual communication**.

My infographic explored the problem of urban transit delays and commuter congestion, presenting the issue through a structured visual story. Instead of overwhelming the audience with paragraphs of information, I transformed the topic into a sequence of key ideas: the commuter bottleneck, the scale of the problem, its causes, its effects, and a possible technology-based solution.

The design combines illustrations, icons, statistics, short explanations, and visual hierarchy to make a complex topic more accessible and social-media friendly.

### ✦ The visual story

**01 — The Problem**
Unpredictable schedules, limited visibility, and terminal overcrowding create a commuter bottleneck.

**02 — The Scale**
More than 240 hours can be lost annually because of traffic delays and stagnant queues, based on the statistic used in the project.

**03 — The Causes**
Unsynchronized fleet dispatching, limited seat-capacity visibility, outdated routes, and peak-hour bottlenecks contribute to transit delays.

**04 — The Impact**
Delays can contribute to fatigue, stress, lost productivity, and increased fuel waste.

**05 — The IT Solution**
A smart IoT fleet-tracking concept introduces real-time GPS tracking, passenger information, and dynamic dispatching.

### ✦ My creative takeaway

This activity taught me that **design can make information easier to remember, easier to understand, and harder to ignore.**

I learned to think not only as a designer, but also as a communicator:

> *What does my audience need to understand first?*
> *What can I simplify?*
> *What deserves emphasis?*

# ⋆｡°✩ ACTIVITY 04 — FROM PROBLEM TO POSSIBILITY ✩°｡⋆

### From Jam-Packed to On Track

<div align="center">

<img width="100%" height="500" alt="GE 4120 PRELIMS_FINDING THE SOLUTION" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3fedbfcf-8452-42b8-874f-3e690908b57a" />

</div>

> *Untangling Urban Commuter Pandemonium With a Cloud-Integrated Transit Telemetry Platform*

This final activity became the most concept-driven part of my Prelim journey.

Building from the transit problem presented in my infographic, I developed **TransitPulse**, a proposed Cloud-Integrated Transit Telemetry and Real-Time Passenger Information System designed to address information gaps within urban transportation.

The project takes the problem one step further. Instead of simply showing what is wrong, I explored what a technology-enabled solution could look like.

TransitPulse connects real-time fleet telemetry, cloud analytics, dynamic dispatching, commuter information, and passenger capacity data into a single ecosystem.

### ✦ The idea behind TransitPulse

**GPS + Passenger Data**
↓
**Cellular Transmission**
↓
**Cloud Analytics**
↓
**Dynamic Dispatch Engine**
↓
**Commuter Information**

The proposed system uses GPS modules and Automated Passenger Counter sensors to capture vehicle location, speed, and passenger occupancy. These data are transmitted to a cloud-based analytics engine that processes the information to generate estimated arrival times, monitor route conditions, and identify overcrowding.

The information is then delivered to both transit operators and commuters through dashboards, mobile interfaces, and digital terminal displays.

### ✦ Target beneficiaries

* Urban commuters and students
* Transit operators and drivers
* Municipal urban planners

### ✦ My creative takeaway

This project changed the way I looked at digital design.

I realized that technology projects do not begin with technology alone. They begin with **a problem worth solving**.

The activity challenged me to connect research, problem identification, technology, communication, and visual presentation into one coherent concept.

It was no longer simply about:

*What can I design?*

It became:

> **What problem can my design help people see, understand, and solve?**
